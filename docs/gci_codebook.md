# GCI Codebook

The full codebook documents all 213 underlying datasets: source links, native
scales, transformations applied, and reliability grades, organised by subindex
and domain.

## What the codebook contains

For each of the eight subindices, and each domain within it, the codebook lists
every dataset with:

- **Dataset** — name, hyperlinked to its original source
- **Description** — what the indicator measures, and for survey items the exact
  question wording
- **Scale** — the native scale of the source data before transformation
- **Transformation** — the normalization applied (typically z-score followed by
  min–max normalization to 0–10 per year)
- **Grade** — a reliability score from 1 to 5, assessed on source methodology,
  transparency, and coverage

It also documents the parabolic scaling applied to two datasets in the Private
Ownership domain, and the Executive Opinion Survey methodology underlying the 81
survey-based inputs.
